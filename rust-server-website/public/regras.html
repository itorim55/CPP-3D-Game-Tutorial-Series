Server rules

Last updated: August 2026 · Not knowing the rules is no excuse. When in doubt, ask on Discord before acting.

1. Cheating and exploits — zero tolerance

  • Any cheat, script, recoil macro or exploit = permanent ban, no warning.
  • Accounts with a VAC or game ban under 180 days old can't play here.
  • Teaming with a known cheater = ban by association.
  • Every ban has recorded evidence and appears on the public list. You can contest it on Discord.

2. Group limit

  • Maximum 4 players per team (base, alliance, roam — everything counts).
  • One member swap allowed per wipe. Announce it on Discord so staff can log it.
  • Alliances between groups to dodge the limit = warning, then base wipe.

3. Toxicity

  • Normal Rust banter is fine — hate speech, racism, real threats and doxxing are not.
  • Voice/chat spam: mute. Repeat offense: temporary ban.
  • Stream sniping content creators = ban.

4. Fair play

  • There are no "safe zones" beyond the game's own — raiding and roof camping are part of Rust.
  • Bug abuse (build glitches, seeing through walls, etc.) = same category as cheating.
  • Selling items/bases for real money (RMT) = ban.

5. Wipes

  • Map wipe: every force wipe Thursday (first Thursday of the month) and a mid-wipe if announced.
  • Blueprint wipe: only on the monthly force wipe.
  • The countdown is always on the home page and on Discord.

6. Staff

  • Staff follow the Moderator Code — zero in-game advantages, evidence for every ban.
  • Reports: use F7 in-game or open a ticket on Discord. All of them get investigated.
  • If you think an admin abused their powers, report it to the owner on Discord — we take that more seriously than anything else.
