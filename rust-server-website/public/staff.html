The Moderator Code

Every team member signs and follows this code. Violations mean immediate removal from the team — no exceptions. Click a rule to read it in full.

1. Zero playing admins. Staff don't play on the server they moderate — not on their main, not on alts. No spawning items, no noclip/vanish for advantage, no peeking into anyone's base "just to look". If the community can't trust the referee, the server dies.
2. Evidence, always. Every verification is screen-recorded. No cheating ban without a recording, and evidence is kept for at least 30 days to answer appeals — published whenever possible, camomo_10 style.
3. Public bans. Every ban appears on the public list above, with the reason and the admin responsible. Anyone can contest any decision — appeal here.
4. Total neutrality. An admin never moderates situations involving their own team/friends — the case goes to another staff member.
5. Respect, always. Even with cheaters and toxic players: professionalism. We represent the server.
