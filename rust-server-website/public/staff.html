The Moderator Code

Every team member signs and follows this code. Violations mean immediate removal from the team — no exceptions.

1. Zero in-game advantages. Admins who play the wipe use a separate account with no powers, or don't play at all. No spawning items on a live wipe, no noclip/vanish for advantage, no peeking into anyone's base "just to look".
2. Evidence, always. No cheating ban without a recording. Evidence is kept and, whenever possible, published — camomo_10 style.
3. Public bans. Every ban appears on the public list below, with the reason and the admin responsible. The community can contest any decision on Discord.
4. Total neutrality. An admin never moderates situations involving their own team/friends — the case goes to another staff member.
5. Respect, always. Even with cheaters and toxic players: professionalism. We represent the server.
