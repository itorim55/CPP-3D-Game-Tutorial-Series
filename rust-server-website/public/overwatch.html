Community Overwatch

The community hunts cheaters too

Staff publish clips of suspicious players here — always anonymous (no names, so there are no witch-hunts). Watch the clip and give your verdict. Community votes don't ban anyone: they're one more signal for the staff investigation, which always decides based on recorded evidence. You need 5 h+ of playtime on the server to vote — and you only see the results after voting, so you're not influenced.

Cases are anonymous by design — seen something suspicious yourself? Report in-game with F7 or on Discord.
